Pull up a rocking chair. Cozy Fall is a slow, warm-hearted decorating sandbox set on a single autumn cottage porch — no timers, no currency, no scores. Just you, a yard full of golden light, and twenty pieces of fall decor waiting to be arranged exactly how you like them.

DECORATE AT YOUR OWN PACE
Pumpkin clusters, hay bales, corn stalk bundles, maple wreaths, brass lanterns, pillar candles, plaid blankets, mums, a garden bench, string lights and more. Browse four collections — Harvest, Porch, Garden and Wall — and place each piece wherever it feels right. Move it, mirror it, or tuck it back into storage whenever the mood changes. Your porch saves itself as you go.

VISITORS COME WHEN THE PORCH FEELS RIGHT
A squirrel stops by the bird feeder. A fox pauses beside the lantern. A rabbit, a raccoon, a black cat and a little white maltipoo all wander in and out through the falling leaves. What you place changes who shows up — hang a feeder and watch who notices — but nobody is ever a task to complete. They simply visit, look around, and amble off again.

AMBIENT MODE
Set the porch down and let it breathe. Ambient Mode hides every menu and leaves only the scene: drifting leaves, shifting afternoon light, and visitors passing through on their own schedule. It is built to be left running — a quiet window on a warm autumn afternoon whenever you want one.

BUILT FOR THE COUCH
Cozy Fall started life on the television, so it plays beautifully with a D-pad or remote as well as a touchscreen. Navigation is deliberately simple: move, select to place, back to cancel. There is nothing to learn and nothing to master.

WHAT THIS GAME DOES NOT HAVE
No accounts. No sign-in. No in-app purchases. No premium currency. No unlocks, energy meters, daily streaks, or anything asking for your attention when you are not playing. Every piece of decor is available from the moment you open it. The app is supported by a single banner ad at the bottom of the screen, which is hidden entirely while you are in Ambient Mode.

Rearrange the pumpkins. Light the lanterns. Watch the leaves come down. Cozy Fall is here whenever you want somewhere calm to be.
